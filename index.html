Full description
One rule: turn every tile blue. Swipe to move. Every tile you step on flips colour. Sounds simple - until the board starts flipping back, and the one route that solves it is the one you almost missed. One More Tile is a minimalist logic puzzle for people who like thinking, not hurrying. No timers. No lives. No pressure. Just you, a small grid, and the quiet satisfaction of a board turning completely blue. HOW IT PLAYS - Swipe up, down, left or right to move to the next tile. - Every tile you land on flips colour: red becomes blue, blue becomes red. - Turn every playable tile blue to solve the board. - Beat the move limit to earn all three stars. A CAMPAIGN THAT KEEPS CHANGING THE RULES Chain tiles flip their neighbours too. Ice tiles carry you onward. Portals teleport you across the board. Twin tiles flip together however far apart they sit. Fragile tiles break the moment you step off them. Springboards carry you clean over whatever lies between. Plus switches, one-way tiles, locked tiles and prisms. A PUZZLE EVERY DAY A fresh Daily Puzzle every day, with a streak to keep alive. BUILT TO BE PLEASANT Plays offline. No account, no sign-up, no social feed. Responsive sound and haptics, both switchable in Settings. A calm dark interface designed for one hand and late evenings. One More Tile is free to play and supported by occasional ads. A rewarded ad can give you three extra moves when you want them - it is always optional and never required to finish a level.

How it was made

Every level in the campaign is authored by hand rather than generated, and a solver in the project proves each one is solvable inside its move limit before it ships. The store screenshots are rendered from the real game widgets and the promotional clips from real game sessions, so the marketing cannot drift from the product. Built solo, in Flutter, in five languages.
